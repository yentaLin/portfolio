## The problem and data

This project focused on identifying and separating nuclei in Ki-67 pathology images. I curated and labeled images for semi-supervised cell segmentation research.

## Implementation

Reproduced **UniMatch**, establishing the strongest baseline across the study experiments, and applied **watershed post-processing** to separate adjacent nuclei.

## Results

The approach achieved **79% mIoU** on study data. This describes segmentation performance in the study and does not establish validation for clinical use or other data distributions.
