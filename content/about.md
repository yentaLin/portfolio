<p class="lead">I’m Yen-Ta at CMU, focusing on embedded ML systems.</p>

My background is in electrical engineering. At National Cheng Kung University, I worked on firmware, CAN communication, and vehicle control with the Formula Racing team. Integrating software with hardware taught me to look beyond the code: one communication failure eventually led me to an open circuit in an adapter cable, traced with an oscilloscope.

## From models to working systems

My research projects have included Ki-67 cell segmentation, aerial search-and-rescue vision, and low-bit quantization for Llama models. Each brought a different combination of data, resource constraints, and evaluation challenges.

At MoBagel, I worked on time-series forecasting, GPU integration with Kubernetes, and Airflow monitoring tools. Performance measurements sometimes supported deployment and sometimes showed that the speedup was insufficient. Both were useful engineering outcomes.

## Current focus

I’m at CMU, focusing on embedded ML systems. I’m interested in how models run under real hardware constraints, and the trade-offs between inference quality, memory, and computation.
